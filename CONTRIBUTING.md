# Contributing

Thanks for your interest. This toolkit improves when practitioners suggest sharper fields, clearer guidance, or corrections against the regulations.

## How to contribute

- **Spotted an error or a stale regulatory reference?** Open an issue with the source.
- **Have a better field, phrasing, or example?** Open a pull request against the relevant template.
- **Adding an Arabic translation?** Keep it as a parallel `-ar` file beside the English source (e.g. `ROPA-template-ar.md`) so the pair stays together.

## Ground rules

1. **Public/open frameworks and synthetic data only.** Never contribute content derived from a specific organisation's internal documents, or any real personal data.
2. **Not legal advice.** Keep contributions framed as practical templates, not legal opinions.
3. **Keep it fillable.** Favour `[bracketed placeholders]` and short inline guidance over prose walls.
4. **License.** By contributing, you agree your contribution is licensed under the repository's [MIT License](LICENSE).
