# Regulatory Currency Status

**Last reviewed: 2026-09-05.**

Compliance material goes stale. This page records what the toolkit assumes about each regime, what is **settled law**, and what is a **provisional reading** that may change. If you are relying on an artefact from this repo, check the date above first.

> This is a practitioner's orientation, **not legal advice**. See [`DISCLAIMER.md`](DISCLAIMER.md).

## UAE PDPL - Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021

| | |
|---|---|
| **Settled** | The law is in force. Its obligations (lawful basis, data-subject rights, security, breach notification, cross-border transfer conditions, DPO in defined cases) are on the statute book. |
| **Not settled** | **The Executive Regulations have not been published.** They were due within six months of the law's issuance in September 2021. As of this review, no primary or official UAE source confirms their publication. |
| **Consequence** | The law provides a further six months from the issuance of the Executive Regulations for organisations to adjust. In practice: the compliance clock has not started, and the detailed operational requirements are not yet knowable. |
| **What this toolkit does** | Builds to the statutory obligations and to GDPR-equivalent practice, on the reasoning that an organisation meeting GDPR-level standards is well positioned when the regulations land. Any field here that anticipates a regulation is marked as such. |

**A caution on sources.** A number of vendor and consultancy pages assert that the Executive Regulations were issued in 2024 or 2026. We were unable to corroborate any of these against a primary source; the UAE Legislation portal entry for the decree-law lists no related implementing legislation. Treat confident claims about the issuance date - including any specific "compliance deadline" built on one - with scepticism, and verify against an official source before acting.

## EU AI Act - Regulation (EU) 2024/1689

| | |
|---|---|
| **Settled** | In force. Prohibited-practice and AI-literacy provisions apply. The risk-tier structure (prohibited / high-risk / limited / minimal) is fixed in the text. |
| **Phasing** | High-risk system obligations phase in on a staged timetable running toward late 2027. Which tier a given system falls into is frequently the hard question, and guidance is still developing. |
| **What this toolkit does** | The AI-system risk checklist is structured around the Act's requirement categories (risk management, data governance, transparency, human oversight, logging, robustness). It helps you evidence a tier decision; it does not make one for you. |

## GDPR - Regulation (EU) 2016/679

| | |
|---|---|
| **Settled** | In force since 2018. The obligations this toolkit maps to (Art. 30 records, Art. 35 DPIA, Arts. 33-34 breach notification, Art. 32 security) are stable and well litigated. |
| **Moves** | Supervisory-authority guidance and case law shift interpretation, particularly on legitimate interests, international transfers, and automated decision-making. |

## Free zones - DIFC and ADGM

Both operate their own data-protection regimes, separate from the federal PDPL, broadly tracking GDPR. **Not yet mapped in this toolkit** - see the roadmap in [`README.md`](README.md). If your entity is in DIFC or ADGM, the federal PDPL mapping here is not the law that applies to you.
