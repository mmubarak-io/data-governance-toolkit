# Glossary

Plain-language definitions of the terms used across the toolkit. Summaries for orientation, **not legal definitions**.

- **Controller** - the entity that decides *why* and *how* personal data is processed. Bears primary accountability.
- **Processor** - an entity that processes personal data on a controller's behalf and instructions (e.g. a SaaS vendor).
- **Personal data** - any information relating to an identified or identifiable person (name, email, ID number, location, online identifiers).
- **Special-category / sensitive data** - data needing extra protection: health, biometric, genetic, racial/ethnic origin, religious or political belief, criminal data. Defaults to the Restricted tier here.
- **Data subject** - the individual whom personal data is about.
- **DPO (Data Protection Officer)** - the person accountable for data-protection compliance; required in defined cases under GDPR and PDPL.
- **ROPA (Record of Processing Activities)** - the register of an organisation's processing activities (GDPR Art. 30).
- **DPIA (Data Protection Impact Assessment)** - a structured assessment of privacy risk for high-risk processing, done before processing begins (GDPR Art. 35).
- **Lawful basis** - the legal justification for processing (e.g. consent, contract, legal obligation, legitimate interests under GDPR Art. 6).
- **Data minimisation** - collecting and keeping only the personal data necessary for the stated purpose.
- **Pseudonymisation** - processing data so it can no longer be attributed to a person without separately-kept additional information (reversible).
- **Anonymisation** - irreversibly preventing identification; truly anonymised data falls outside data-protection law.
- **Masking / tokenisation** - replacing a sensitive value with a redacted or surrogate value (e.g. showing only the last 4 digits of an IBAN).
- **Cross-border transfer** - moving personal data to another jurisdiction; restricted unless a safeguard (adequacy, SCCs, or a PDPL transfer condition) applies.
- **SCCs (Standard Contractual Clauses)** - pre-approved contract terms that provide a transfer safeguard under GDPR.
- **Adequacy** - a determination that a country provides an adequate level of data protection, permitting transfers without extra safeguards.
- **Classification tier** - the sensitivity label (Public / Internal / Confidential / Restricted) that drives handling controls.
- **High-risk AI system** - under the EU AI Act, an AI system in a sensitive domain (Annex III) subject to the heaviest obligations.
- **GPAI (General-Purpose AI)** - foundation/large models subject to provider transparency duties under the EU AI Act.
- **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)** - an architecture where an LLM answers using retrieved source documents; relevant to citation tracking and audit logging.
- **PDPL** - the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021).
- **Data Office** - the UAE federal authority overseeing PDPL.
