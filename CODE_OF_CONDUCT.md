# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be straightforward and be kind. This is a small toolkit maintained in spare time by one
person, and it improves when practitioners disagree well.

## Expected

- Argue with the content, not the contributor. "This misreads Art. 33" is useful; a swipe
  at the person is not.
- Bring a source. Corrections about a regulation land best with a link to the primary text.
- Assume the maintainer and other contributors are working in good faith and may be in a
  different jurisdiction, language, or regulatory context than you.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, and sustained disruption. Also:
posting real personal data, or content taken from an organisation's internal documents.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainer through
[GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/mmubarak-io/data-governance-toolkit/security/advisories/new)
or by opening an issue if it is not sensitive. The maintainer may edit, hide, or remove
contributions, and block accounts, for behaviour that breaches this document.
